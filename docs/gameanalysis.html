SACRIFICES MUST BE MADE

Look and Sound

Ambient low-frequency background music underlies board-game and card sound effects. Visually you see only the dark and the stranger's eyes — nothing of the surroundings. The mystery is intentional. Graphically it sits around N64/GameCube era aesthetics.

Setting

A cabin in the middle of nowhere — you, your child, and The Man. We know this only because he tells us. All we see is the table and the card game. The setting's power comes entirely from what's withheld.

Characters

Two characters: the player, and The Man (Leshy in the full release, Inscryption). Leshy is creepy, omniscient, and delights in the mystery. Your child exists only as a story element — never seen, never heard. The game's hook is everything you don't know.

Challenge

A card game requiring strategic sacrifice. Difficulty scales each level. Dying restarts the round — punishment is light, but getting stuck is easy depending on your playstyle.
