Sacrifices Must Be Made is the game that later turned into Inscryption. You sit in a cabin across the table from the hunter and play a card game against him with animal cards.

The weakest card is the squirrel. It does not do much on its own, but you can sacrifice it to pay the blood cost of a stronger card like the stoat, the wolf, or the grizzly.

Flying cards like the raven and the hawk can attack past the cards in front of them. When the player loses, the hunter keeps going with his story, and the game gets darker the longer you play.
